1. Problem Statement
Customer Churn Prediction
A telecomsubscription company wants to identify customers who are likely to leave its service.

Customer churn occurs when a customer cancels their subscription or switches to another service provider. If the company can predict churn in advance, it can take preventive actions such as offering discounts, improving customer support, providing personalized plans, or contacting high-risk customers.

The objective of this project is to develop a Machine Learning classification system that analyzes customer information such as
- Customer tenure
- Monthly charges
- Total charges
- Contract type
- Internet service
- Payment method
- Technical support
- Online security
- Demographic information
- Service usage
and predicts
0 → Customer will stay
1 → Customer will churn

The system will also generate a churn probability and classify customers into
Low Risk
Medium Risk
High Risk

Machine Learning Problem
Type Supervised Learning
Task Binary Classification
Target Churn
Expected Business Output
For example
Customer ID C10025

Churn Probability 82.6%
Prediction Churn
Risk Level High Risk

This allows the company to prioritize high-risk customers for retention campaigns.
